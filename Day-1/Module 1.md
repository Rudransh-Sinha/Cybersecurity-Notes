***Types Of Network***

1} Local Network :- In this there can be either two devices connected to each other or can be hundreds of thousands of computer connected to each other.
-SH:- Small Home network connects a few computers to each other and to interact.
-SOHO:- Small Office Home Office allows computers in a home office or remote office to connect to a corporate network or access centralized and shared resources.
-M2L:- Multiple places with Multiple Thousand of Computers connected with each other.
-WWN:- Network of networks that connects hundreds of millions of computers world wide.

***Types Of Data:-***

1} Volunteer Data:- Data about yourself
2} Inferred Data:- Generates by activity for e.g when you use credit card your preferences, like dislikes and location.
3} Observed Data:- Captured by recording the actions of individuals .

 ***Bandwidth***
 It is the capacity of a medium to carry data. Digital bandwidth measures the amount of data that can flow from one place to another in a given amount of time.
 ***Throughput***
 It is the amount of data sent and received over a connection , including any delays that might occur.

        Usually at home , download speed is higher than upload speed.
